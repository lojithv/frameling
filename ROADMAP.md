# Roadmap

What's planned for Frameling, in order. Each item lands as its own commit and
works in the editor, in the web export and in the desktop MP4 export, for every
frame shape (16:9, 9:16 and square).

## Creator essentials (done)

Ranked by how much they help someone make a finished short video quickly.

- [x] **Lip sync with mouth shapes.** Characters form real mouth shapes (closed
      M/B/P, F/V, O, OO, wide vowels, teeth, tongue) timed to the words of each
      voice line, not just open and shut. Two mouth sets for now (classic and
      lips), stick figures included, with a per-character mode and a keyable
      mouth track to fix any frame by hand.
- [x] **Background music and sound effects with ducking.** A music track for the
      whole movie that gets quieter under voices (with fades and looping), plus
      a built-in library of original sound effects (whooshes, pops, dings...).
      Built-in music and sounds are generated by Frameling itself (CC0).
- [x] **Templates.** Ready-made projects such as a two-person chat, a story
      explainer, a reaction video and a podcast, in 9:16 and 16:9, so a video
      takes minutes.
- [x] **Camera moves.** One-click zoom punches, shakes, push-ins, pans and quick
      cuts at the playhead, and an auto camera that cuts to whoever is talking.
- [x] **Backgrounds and props packs, and image import.** More original rooms,
      streets, a classroom and outdoor scenes and props, plus your own images as
      backgrounds or items on stage.
- [x] **Script to video.** Paste a dialogue script in the editor and get the
      cast, voices, lip sync, subtitles and a rough animation generated.
- [x] **Export presets.** One-click exports for vertical shorts, YouTube and
      square posts, with subtitles burned in, plus a thumbnail image.

## Later

- GPU renderer (Vello on wgpu) behind the same display list, for 4K and heavy scenes.
- WASM plugins (Extism) that receive commands and can register effects and importers.
- Phoneme-accurate lip sync from the durations the TTS model predicts.
- Zip project format (`.frameling` = zip of JSON + media) for large audio.
- Live mode for animated podcasts and streaming (see the design doc).
